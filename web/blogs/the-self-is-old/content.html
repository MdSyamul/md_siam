Quiet Ruin of the Self

The self is old—older than the universe.

Have you ever noticed that, in this ever-changing universe, you are the only thing that seems constant?

✦

You have just stepped into your thirties, yet you feel much the same as you did at twelve. You used to run across the football field until you could no longer catch your breath; today, you cannot remember the last time you stepped onto one.

The things you once held dear—and the things you feared—are no longer the same. ​ Strangely, something within you still feels familiar, quietly witnessing every version of you—experiencing the ever-changing universe.

This awareness is the self.

✦

If I had to name one truth I have learned in my thirty years of life, it would be this: one’s purpose in life is the self—to untether it, understand it, and guide it toward stillness: a mind calm within, composed in response, and fully present as life unfolds.

The purpose is the self.

✦

Never doubt that the self is worthy of pursuit. I once read a brief passage—though I no longer remember its exact wording—that has stayed with me: You are the instrument through which the universe sees itself; you are the way it is coming to understand itself. When you doubt, it doubts; when you care, it cares.

The universe is not indifferent; it responds to the self and evolves with every act of awareness. What, then, could be more worthy of pursuit than the self?

The universe is the self.

✦✦✦

If you are one of my students—seeing no purpose in the struggles of this brief life—perhaps there is none; perhaps it remains beyond our knowing. However, I hope these words help. I wish you the very best.
